Privacy policy

Family Hub. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Family Hub is a private app used only by the Tyler-Hennessy family on their own Mac. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What Google data it uses

  • Google Calendar: it reads the family's calendars and events so they can be shown on screen, and creates, changes or deletes events only when a family member does so in the app.
  • Google Drive (read-only): it lists the family's folders so they can pick one, and downloads the photos in that one chosen folder so they can be shown as a slideshow. It never changes or deletes anything in Google Drive.

Where the data goes

  • Everything stays on the family's own Mac. There is no Family Hub server, and no data is sent to anyone other than Google.
  • The sign-in is stored in the Mac's Keychain.
  • Photos and a copy of upcoming calendar events are kept on the Mac so the screen still works if the internet drops. Photos removed from the Drive folder are removed from the Mac too.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model.

Other services

  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo.com. Only the town's location is sent, never any Google data.
  • Photo locations are shown using Apple Maps on the Mac. No Google data is sent to Apple.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use "Sign out" in Family Hub's Settings, or remove Family Hub at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app from the Mac removes all of its saved data.
