Family Hub

Family Hub is a private Mac app made for one household, the Tyler-Hennessy family. It runs on the family's kitchen Mac as a digital photo frame and family calendar. It is not sold or offered to the public.

What it does

  • Shows a slideshow of photos from one Google Drive folder that the family shares.
  • Shows the family's Google Calendar, and lets family members add, change and delete events from the kitchen.
  • Shows the time, date and local weather.

How it uses Google

Family Hub signs in with the family's own Google account so it can read and update their calendar and read photos from their chosen Drive folder. It does not access anyone else's account. See the privacy policy for details.
